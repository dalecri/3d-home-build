# Exterior reference photos (described in text)

The client sent these 5 exterior photos inline only, so they are not on disk. These descriptions are the
reference for the `front`, `balcony`, `patio`, `chalet` and `shed` views. Late spring, bright sun, blue sky.

## front (listing photo 1/41 "Frontage") - house seen from the gravel driveway, looking south
- Single-storey cottage, walls clad in orange-tan **cedar shakes**. Trim, corner boards and window frames
  are a **saturated teal-blue** (~#2d6a85). Roof: **grey asphalt shingles**, white aluminium fascia/soffit.
- Left ~60% of the facade: a **screened 3-season porch** projecting forward: teal-blue posts dividing it into
  5-6 tall screened bays above a low shingled knee wall (~0.5 m). Through the screens you see a wicker
  chair, hanging flower baskets (pink/red petunias), a sun hat. Porch has its own lower, shallow roof whose
  front edge is level with the main eave.
- A **white Victorian screen door** with an **oval cut-out** in the upper half sits right-of-centre on the
  porch front, reached by a short flagstone path.
- Right ~40%: a shingled wing with a **front-facing gable** (white fascia, small wall vent near the peak)
  and one small window with teal-blue frame. A teal downspout on the right corner.
- Roof: black metal chimney pipe on the left-centre of the main roof, a squat black chimney stack in the
  middle, a **satellite dish** on a pole at the right-centre of the roof line, TV antenna mast.
- Two big **white birch trunks** stand just right of the screen door, in front of the gable wing.
- Low leafy green shrubs (spirea) along the whole front, daylilies right. Grass/dirt clearing + gravel in
  foreground. Far left, partly visible: the patio with a wood bench and a teal Adirondack chair.
- Background: very tall spruce/pine and leafy deciduous trees, forested hill behind.

## balcony (listing photo 15/41) - on the lake-side deck, looking along the house wall
- Wide deck (~2.5 m) of weathered **cedar-brown pressure-treated boards** running along the house.
- Railing: chunky square posts every ~1.8 m, top cap rail, a bottom rail, closely spaced square balusters,
  all the same warm orange-brown wood. The deck is high up: you look **into tree canopy** (pines, spruce,
  birch, maples) and through the trunks glimpse the lake. No ground visible beyond the rail.
- Left side: house wall with cedar shakes, teal-blue trim around a white sliding door/window, white
  aluminium soffit and gutter overhead, a small security light.
- Three **bright green plastic Adirondack chairs** in a row against the house wall, facing the rail,
  and a small glass side table.
- The deck continues around a corner at the far end with the same railing.

## patio (listing photo 12/41) - square paver patio in a mown clearing
- Roughly round patch of **grey square concrete pavers** (~60 cm), with grass in the joints, uneven edges.
- Centre: a **rusty copper-brown square fire pit** with a lattice/criss-cross pattern and a pyramidal screen lid.
- Behind it: an old weathered **outdoor fireplace/barbecue** (grey stone/concrete box) with a tall black
  **stovepipe** rising ~2 m.
- Right-behind: a low **wooden bench** with plank back (brown weathered), and two stacked
  **turquoise/teal Adirondack chairs** next to it. A short concrete block wall.
- Left foreground: a stone-edged flower bed with a big clump of daylily leaves; a white round planter and a
  small wooden planter box on the grass.
- Background: wall of tall spruces/pines with leafy shrubs below; a **crabapple with pink-magenta blossoms**
  at the far left edge. Bright blue sky.

## chalet (listing photo 10/41 "Petit chalet d'invite 3 saisons") - small guest cabin in the woods
- Tiny cabin (~3 x 3.5 m) with **pale aqua/mint horizontal lap siding**, steep **gable roof with dark
  brown edge trim**, gable facing the viewer.
- Front: a **white door with a multi-pane glass upper**, and to its left a wide window with dark brown
  frame (screened, 3-season). Small low wooden deck across the front holding a **black wrought-iron bistro
  set** (round table + 2 chairs).
- A wooden **lattice trellis** panel stands in the clearing to the left of the cabin.
- Setting: lush green ground cover (wild lily-of-the-valley), surrounded by birches, spruces and young
  maples. Behind the cabin to the right you glimpse **another small shed with a blue door** and something
  yellow/orange (kayak).

## shed (listing photo 11/41 "Shed") - shed near the water
- Small gable shed, **white door** with **blue trim**, front walls hidden under a huge mass of
  **dry brown climbing vines** (dead Virginia creeper / grape) that covers the walls and part of the roof.
- **White lattice** skirt visible at the lower left side through the vines. Blue-trimmed roof edge.
- A small wooden step/pallet at the door.
- To the right: a **large crabapple tree** in full **pink/magenta bloom**, a round daylily bed edged with
  stones, a tree stump. Behind: a **wire-mesh raised garden enclosure** and the corner of another white
  building (the chalet's neighbour shed).
- Mown grass foreground, forest of spruce and birch behind.
